Descriptive Method
▪ Systematic approach used to observe, describe, and document aspects of human behaviour,
phenomena, or populations as they naturally occur
▪ Key Feature: Focus on observations
▪ Types of descriptive research
▪ Naturalistic observation
▪ Laboratory observation
▪ Clinical case study
▪ Survey
▪ Cross-sectional and Longitudinal research
15/01/2026 HS 110 | Introduction to Psychology 4
Descriptive Method: Naturalistic Observation
▪ Behaviour(s) of people and animal in their natural environment
▪ Observer effect: tendency to behave differently when ‘they’ know that they are being
observed
▪ Participant observation: researcher becomes a participant in the group being observed
▪ Strength:
▪ First-hand look
▪ Drawbacks:
▪ Observer bias: tendency of researchers to see what they expect to see
“What is happening out there?”
▪ Hawthorne effect: Increase in effective behaviour when being observed
▪ Cannot be replicated
(Ciccarelli & Meyer, 2006)
