Primary or core emotions
How do I identify my emotion(s)?
1. What is the context/situation/person?
2. What am I feeling?
3. Allow yourself to feel your feelings. Sit with it
4. Notice the physical sensations in your body
(i.e., pressure on chest, stomachache, headache)
5. Then identify whether you are experiencing a
primary or secondary emotion
