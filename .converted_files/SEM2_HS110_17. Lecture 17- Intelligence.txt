History of testing
Types of Cognitive ability testing
• Ability tests (Present):
• Assess a person’s innate or general cognitive capacity
• Natural capability in specific skills
• For example: Wechsler Adult Intelligence Scale (WAIS)
• Aptitude tests (Future):
• Measures the potential and capacity to learn new skills
• Persons ability to acquire knowledge or skills that have not been
learned
• For example: DAT
• Achievement tests (Past): Speed test
Power test
(time limit)
• Evaluate what individuals have already learned or accomplished (increasing
difficulty)
• Knowledge and skill gained through instructions or experience
• For example: College exams
