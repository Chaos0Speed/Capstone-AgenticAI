Theories of Intelligence: Cattell’s Theory of Intelligence (cont’d…)
Criticism:
▪ The theory oversimplifies human intelligence by dividing it into
only two types
▪ Fluid and crystallized intelligence are interrelated, not completely
separate
▪ It focuses on intellectual abilities, ignoring creativity, emotional,
and practical intelligence
